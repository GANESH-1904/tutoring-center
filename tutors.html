Questions about our tutors

Quick answers before you call us.

Can I meet the tutor before enrolling?

Yes. Your free demo class is taught by the tutor your child would have, and you can chat with them afterwards.

Will my child keep the same tutor?

Yes, unless you ask for a change. Tutors stay with a batch through the term.

Which boards do you cover?

CBSE, ICSE and Tamil Nadu State Board, plus Olympiad practice.

How do I follow my child’s progress?

Attendance, reports and fees are in the parent dashboard.

Can I change batches later?

Yes, at the start of any month.
