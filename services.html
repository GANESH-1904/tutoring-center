What we offer

Six ways we support your child

Mix and match. Most families combine group batches with weekly doubt sessions.

Group batches

Grades 1–12 · 4 classes a week

Small groups of 8–10 by grade, taught by a subject specialist. Best for steady weekly progress.

1:1 tutoring

Flexible hours · 45 min sessions

A personal tutor builds a plan around your child’s gaps. Ideal for catching up or going ahead.

Exam preparation

Mock tests every month

Board, Olympiad and Class 10/12 practice with timed papers and marked feedback.

Homework help

Mon–Fri · 1 hour

A supervised daily hour so homework is finished, understood and never a bedtime battle.

Progress reports

Every Friday

Marks, attendance and tutor notes sent to parents every week in the dashboard.

Doubt clearing

Reply within 24 hours

Post a question any evening and get a worked answer by next morning, plus free weekend sessions.

Plans and fees

Quick answers before you call us.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Every new student gets one free demo class with no commitment.

Which boards do you cover?

CBSE, ICSE and Tamil Nadu State Board, plus Olympiad practice.

Can I change batches later?

Yes, at the start of any month.

How do I follow my child’s progress?

Attendance, reports and fees are in the parent dashboard.

How to pay fees

Four easy ways. Every payment gets a digital receipt.

UPI

Pay from any UPI app and get an instant receipt in the dashboard.

Cards and net banking

Secure online payment from the dashboard. Debit, credit and net banking accepted.

Cash at the center

Pay at the front desk during opening hours. A printed receipt is given on the spot.

Auto-pay

Set up auto-pay once and never miss the 10th. Cancel any time from the dashboard.
