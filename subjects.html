Our method

How we teach each subject

Different subjects need different methods.

Maths

Concept, worked example, then timed practice. Every mistake is reviewed the same day.

  • Timed practice set every class
  • Mistake log reviewed with the tutor
  • Weekly test with a score trend

Science

Simple experiments before theory, so students see the idea first and the formula second.

  • Hands-on demos in the lab
  • Diagrams and flow charts for revision
  • Chapter quizzes before every test

English

Reading, writing and speaking every week, with feedback on one piece of writing.

  • One graded piece of writing each week
  • Speaking circles to build confidence
  • Vocabulary built from stories

Tips from our tutors

Study habits that help at home

Small routines our tutors recommend for each subject.

Maths: practise a little daily

Twenty minutes of problems every day beats two hours before a test.

English: read every day

Any book counts. Reading widely is the quickest way to better writing.

Science: explain it aloud

If your child can teach an idea to someone else, they have understood it.
