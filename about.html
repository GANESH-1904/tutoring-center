Our story

Started with five students and one table

BrightPath began in 2014 when a group of teachers wanted a calmer, more personal way to support school students. Today we teach over 1,200 learners across 12 grades, with the same promise: no child left behind.

  • 2014: founded by school teachers in Velachery
  • 2018: second campus opened to meet demand
  • 2022: parent dashboard launched for weekly reports
  • 2026: 1,200+ students and 45+ verified tutors
Our values

What guides us

Four beliefs behind every class we run.

Care first

We know every student by name, along with their strengths and what makes them nervous.

Clear goals

Every batch has measurable outcomes: a weekly test, a monthly target and a term review with parents.

Safe space

Questions are always welcome. No child is ever laughed at for not knowing something yet.

Growth mindset

Mistakes are how we learn. Effort is praised first, marks second.

Who we help

Is BrightPath right for your child?

Most families come to us for one of these four reasons.

Building confidence

For children who know the answer but hesitate to speak. Small groups and gentle encouragement help them find their voice.

Catching up in one subject

Weekend doubt clinics and a same-day review of mistakes close gaps before they grow.

Preparing for boards

Timed practice, mock tests and writing technique for Grades 9–12.

Aiming higher

Advanced problem sets and mock rounds for Olympiads and NTSE.

Questions parents ask

Quick answers before you call us.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Every new student gets one free demo class with no commitment.

Which boards do you cover?

CBSE, ICSE and Tamil Nadu State Board, plus Olympiad practice.

How big are the batches?

Eight to ten students, so every child gets time with the tutor.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Tutor notes go into the parent dashboard every week, and you get a message when your child arrives and leaves.

Can I change batches later?

Yes, at the start of any month.
