How to support learning at home

Simple habits that make tutoring work better.

  • Sleep: children aged 6–12 need 9–11 hours. Fix a bedtime.
  • Screen time: keep screens off during homework and one hour before bed.
  • Food and water: a light snack before class helps focus.
  • Talk daily: ask “What did you enjoy learning today?”, not “How were your marks?”
